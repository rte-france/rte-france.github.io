Debian is a free and open-source operating system, developed by a worldwide community of volunteers since 1993.

RTE contributes to Debian by submitting patches for issues detected while working on the [SEAPATH project](https://opensource.rte-france.com/projects/seapath).

Examples:
 - [lvm2: activate incomplete VGs in degraded mode late in the initramfs](https://salsa.debian.org/lvm-team/lvm2/-/merge_requests/26)
 - [linux: re-enable KVM on arm64 real-time kernels](https://salsa.debian.org/kernel-team/linux/-/merge_requests/1347)
 - [linux: enable Advantech Embedded Controller watchdog support](https://salsa.debian.org/kernel-team/linux/-/merge_requests/841)
 - [linux: enable PRP/HSR protocols as modules](https://salsa.debian.org/kernel-team/linux/-/merge_requests/682)
